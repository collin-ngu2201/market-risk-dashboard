Backtest: a daily-bar mirror of the live engine over the past year (Yahoo serves too little 30-minute history to backtest intraday). At each day it checks whether the ticker was in a dip entering the session (depth ≥ 1.75× its own 20-day volatility below the 20-day high) and then had a strong breakout day, entering at the close with the same volatility-scaled target/stop; a 15-bar time-stop closes anything that never reaches a level.
